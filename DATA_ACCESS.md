# Data Access and Licensing

## Included in this repository

Scene metadata, split definitions, and machine-readable result tables are
released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
(CC BY 4.0).

## Separately hosted original data

The synthetic nighttime deer dataset and trained checkpoints are not stored in
Git. Their archive URL will be added here after upload:

`DATA_ARCHIVE_URL_TO_ADD`

The dataset archive must include:

- the dataset README and `SCENES.csv`;
- the experiment split files;
- file checksums;
- checkpoint names and hashes;
- a third-party asset/license inventory; and
- explicit reuse terms for rendered images and annotations.

Do not assign an open license to rendered data until the licenses of all Blender
models, textures, vegetation, vehicles, and animal assets have been reviewed.

## Third-party data

nuScenes and other restricted third-party images are not redistributed. Users
must obtain them from their original providers and comply with the corresponding
licenses and terms.
